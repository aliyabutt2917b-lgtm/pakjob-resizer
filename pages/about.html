About Our Utility Portal

PakJob Resizer is a free, fast, and privacy-focused utility engineered to solve application photo rejection issues across Pakistani government job portals.

⚡ Precise Compression

Compress photos strictly under 20KB, 25KB, or 50KB matching PPSC, FPSC, ETEA, and NTS rules.

🔒 100% Private

Your photos never touch a remote server. All resizing happens right inside your phone or browser.

Our Mission

Applying for government vacancies or university admissions often involves strict file size requirements (e.g., 150x200 pixels, under 20KB). Candidates frequently face technical errors or end up paying local internet cafes. We built this automated portal to give every candidate access to free, instantaneous, professional-grade image adjustments paired with actionable recruitment guides.
